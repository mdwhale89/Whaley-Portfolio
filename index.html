Mission Control: The Creative Pipeline

From Analog Sketch to Digital Frame and Physical Fabric

Welcome to the creative nexus of TookStarz Designs. My work exists at the intersection of discipline and imagination—grounded in classical graphite studies and color theory, expanded through vector motion pipelines, and finalized with physical apparel fabrication. Every asset is built with deliberate visual logic, structural weight, and real-world execution in mind.
