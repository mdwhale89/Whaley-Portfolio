DTFYourself Internship: Convention Apparel Study

Direct-to-Film Technical Pipeline & Garment Fabrication

During my internship with DTFYourself, I was commissioned to manage the end-to-end production of two contrasting apparel projects to coincide with the Critical Role Live show at Radio City Music Hall and New York Comic Con. This dual-assignment served as a rigorous technical study of the Direct-to-Film (DTF) pipeline—testing how complex digital narratives translate into high-durability, convention-ready physical assets.

The Technical Pipeline

  • Digital Output & Layering: Designs were mirrored and printed onto specialized PET (polyethylene terephthalate) film. A critical technical step involved calibrating a high-density White ink underbase beneath the CMYK channels, ensuring rich opacity and vibrant color saturation across dark fabric substrates.
  • Adhesive Engineering: TPU (thermoplastic polyurethane) powder was evenly applied to the wet ink layers, acting as the primary chemical bridge facilitating a permanent mechanical bond between ink pigments and textile fibers.
  • Thermal Bonding & Cure: Films were cured through calibrated oven heating to stabilize the adhesive matrix, then fused via high-pressure heat press to ensure industrial-grade wash fastness and stretch resilience under active convention wear.

Throughout this internship, I handled pre-press file setup, color profiling, and rigorous quality control—balancing ink density and thermal press variables to ensure every custom transfer left the shop meeting professional durability standards.

The first assignment focused on Symbolic Synthesis to celebrate the in-game wedding of characters Fjord and Jester. Designed as a direct, personal tribute for two of the cast members, I digitally fabricated a commemorative back-piece by rotoscoping the real-world voice actors into their character counterparts, framed by their iconic weapons and a stylized "Happily Ever After" banner.

Front-Facing Symbolic Geometry

The technical core of the front chest graphic featured two custom-engineered D20 dice housed within interlocking wedding bands. Every pixel served the narrative lore:

  • Naval & Character Motifs: A subtle sea-foam green ship within the dice geometry honored Fjord's maritime history, paired alongside Jester's signature pink-sprinkled donut.
  • Community Easter Egg: The ring setting features an embedded "Fire Resistant Gem"—a precise, lore-driven callback engineered specifically for community recognition.
  • Print Calibration: Translating these tight vector lines, shaded metallic ring bands, and delicate color gradients required precise pre-press ink limiting and white-layer chokes to prevent fine linework from bleeding or blowing out during thermal bonding.

Assignment 2: New York Comic Con

Information Architecture & High-Density Typography

The second phase shifted from symbolic design to Information Architecture. Inspired by the community's culture of satirical "cast shirts," I engineered a spoof featuring Dungeon Master Matt Mercer. While the front utilized a comedic performance asset (Mercer as Gandalf), the back presented a high-density typographic challenge.

Archival Errata Typography

I curated a massive volume of "archival errata"—quotes, running gags, and mispronunciations spanning across three campaigns—and organized them into a structured, highly legible two-tone grid:

  • Micro-Typography & Kerning: Structuring a vast volume of textual data into a cohesive layout required meticulous management of line-spacing, tracking, and optical kerning to prevent visual crowding.
  • Two-Tone Color Separation: Calibrated the Red and Gold palette specifically so adjacent characters would remain crisp, vibrant, and legible against a dark textile backdrop without mutual bleeding.
  • Thermal Cure Fidelity: Fine-tuned the TPU powder coverage and press temperature to ensure the delicate serifs and thin stems of the letterforms did not crack or distort under heat activation, turning an expansive "wall of text" into a durable, professional-grade brand mark.
