The Multi-Disciplinary Pipeline

From Sketchbook to Screen and Fabric

I am a multimedia designer, digital animator, and fabricator specializing in end-to-end creative production. My approach combines traditional fine art discipline with modern digital toolsets, ensuring that every asset—whether a vector illustration, a 2D animation cycle, or a physical Direct-to-Film transfer—is backed by an understanding of light, form, and composition.

Under the moniker TookStarz, I bring that same creative passion to live streaming and community content creation. This real-time interaction keeps my instincts sharp for rhythm, pacing, and audience engagement, informing both my digital branding projects and personal studio works.

Beyond the Screen

Worldbuilding, Technical Rigs & Analog Passions

When I'm not animating frames or managing print runs, my world revolves around creative storytelling and technical builds. I run table-top tabletop campaigns, design custom PC rigs, and explore tactile craftsmanship through physical sketching and traditional printmaking.
