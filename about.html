About the Designer

Digital architect, visual storyteller, and physical fabrication specialist.

Design to Production

Bridging Digital Systems & Physical Assets

I provide a comprehensive design-to-production pipeline, specializing in Digital-to-Film Graphic Design and High-Fidelity 3D Animation. By combining a deep technical understanding of 3D modeling with a focus on brand identity, I translate complex digital concepts into cohesive visual systems—extending from the screen into the physical world through Precision 3D Printing and prototyping.

Whether calibrating multi-channel white ink underbases for commercial textile transfers or optimizing polygon topology and CAD tolerances for physical fabrication, my workflow is built around execution without compromise between creative vision and manufacturing reality.

Beyond the Canvas

Narrative Lore, Worldbuilding & Hardware

My visual identity is shaped by a lifelong obsession with immersive storytelling. When I'm not dialing in vector paths or running render passes in Blender, you can usually find me designing narrative campaigns behind the Dungeon Master screen, dissecting vintage comic book illustration and manga linework, or fine-tuning custom desktop hardware builds.

That same dedication to detailed lore, mechanical balance, and community culture informs every design system I build. I treat branding not just as surface aesthetics, but as an interactive world that audiences and users want to inhabit.

At a Glance

  • Digital Toolkit: Adobe Creative Suite (Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign), Blender, Tinkercad, and Framer.
  • Fabrication Specialties: Direct-to-Film (DTF) apparel printing, Dye-Sublimation performance wear, and FDM/Resin 3D printing.
  • Creative Fuel: Tabletop RPGs & character lore, narrative comic art, mechanical keyboards, and content creation across the TookStarz network.
