About the Designer

Digital architect, visual storyteller, and physical fabrication specialist.

Design to Production

Bridging Digital Systems & Physical Assets

I provide a comprehensive design-to-production pipeline, specializing in Digital-to-Film Graphic Design and High-Fidelity 3D Animation. By combining a deep technical understanding of 3D modeling with a focus on brand identity, I translate complex digital concepts into cohesive visual systems—extending from the screen into the physical world through Precision 3D Printing and prototyping.

Whether calibrating multi-channel white ink underbases for commercial textile transfers or optimizing polygon topology and CAD tolerances for physical fabrication, my workflow is built around execution without compromise between creative vision and manufacturing reality.

Beyond the Screen

Narrative Lore, Worldbuilding & Hardware

My visual identity is fueled by a lifelong connection to immersive storytelling and digital community. When I'm not dialing in vector paths or running render passes in Blender, you can usually find me behind the Dungeon Master screen building custom campaigns, dissecting comic book art and dynamic character illustration, streaming content, or fine-tuning custom desktop PC builds.

That same dedication to detailed lore, mechanical balance, and community culture informs every design system I build. I treat branding not just as surface aesthetics, but as an interactive experience that audiences want to connect with.

At a Glance

  • Core Software: Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign, Blender, Tinkercad, and Framer.
  • Technical Production: Direct-to-Film (DTF) apparel printing, Dye-Sublimation performance wear, and FDM/Resin 3D printing.
  • Creative Focus: Tabletop narrative design, brand merchandise architecture, hardware enthusiast setups, and live streaming across the TookStarz channels.
